# CSC110 In- class Activity: Aunt's Shop Checkout - Conditionals 

## Overall Task
Students are the cashiers at my aunt's shop. Their goal is to calculate the final order price by evaluating a list of regular customers against specific pricing rules. Because the final outcome depends on who is buying and how much they spend, you will need to use conditional logic (if/else statements) to check each order,  applying a discount for eligible regular customers, or awarding a free coupon if the total purchase exceeds a certain amount or just selling at the stated price when no special conditions are met.
